Hi, I'm Matt.

I fix software problems for customers. After hours I build websites, and now games.

The short version

I've spent five years on the technical side of customer experience. I'm the person you reach when the software breaks, who works out why and stays on it until it's fixed.

Four of those years were at Maven Clinic, a telehealth company, where I worked the overnight shift as the only support agent online and was promoted twice. These days I'm a floor lead and Tech Guru on Minted's seasonal team.

The web design started as a hobby and turned into a second job I don't get paid for. This site is my favorite thing I've built so far.

What I'm good at

  • Untangling technical problems. Reproducing the bug, writing it up so an engineer can act on it, and keeping the customer in the loop.
  • Staying calm when things are on fire. I ran customer communication through a major AWS outage as the senior-most person online.
  • Getting teams to use AI tools. I led my team's rollout of Guru AI and built an internal tool for reviewing support conversations.
  • Building for the web. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, shipped on GitHub Pages. No frameworks were harmed in the making of this site.

Why games?

A certain math-games website got a lot of us through the school day. This is my nod to it: somewhere you go on purpose, play for five minutes, and leave feeling a little better.

There are no ads here, no accounts and no feed. Your high scores stay in your own browser.
