How to play

One button. Tap the screen, click, or press Space at the moment the logo lands in a corner.

  • Closer is better. A perfect tap is worth 3 points, a near one 2, a loose one 1.
  • Three hearts. A tap that isn't on a corner costs one. So does a corner you let go by.
  • It gets faster with every corner you catch, and the timing gets tighter.
  • Watch for near misses. After a few corners, some bounces land just beside one. Leave those alone.

On a keyboard, P pauses and M mutes.

Why this exists

Everyone who has waited on a slow computer has watched a logo drift toward a corner and felt something when it finally hit. This is that feeling, on purpose.
