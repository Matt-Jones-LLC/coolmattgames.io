How to play

Tap or click a bubble to pop it. Every ticket that floats off the top costs a heart, and you have five. Pop without missing to build a combo, up to five times the points.

  • Ticket. One tap. 10 points.
  • VIP. Small and fast. 30 points.
  • Escalation. Three taps, and each one knocks it back down. 50 points.
  • Spam. Leave it alone. Tapping it costs a heart.
  • Coffee. Slows everything down for a few seconds.
  • Macro. Clears every ticket and VIP on screen.

On a keyboard, P pauses and M mutes.

Based on a true story

Matt worked the overnight support shift at a telehealth company for four years, as the only agent online. The real queue had fewer bubbles and more coffee.
