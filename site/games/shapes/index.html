SHAPES

How to play

You start as a dot. Tap where you want to walk, or use the arrow keys. Bump into another shape to fight it.

  • Win fights to earn XP. Level up and your dot gets bigger and stronger.
  • Beat each area's boss and a new dot joins you. Two dots make a line, three a triangle, four a square, five a pentagon.
  • Charge your Shape move by giving and taking hits. The more corners you have, the harder it lands.
  • Walk into the Inn to rest and the Shop to buy items and sharpen up.

The road runs east: Dot Central, Linetown, Trianglewood, Squaresville, Dodecahedown Town.

Saving

Your Cool Matt account is a name kept in this browser. The game saves by itself after every battle.

To carry on from another phone or computer, open Account in the game, copy your save code, and load it there.
